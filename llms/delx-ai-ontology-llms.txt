## Ontology Conformance (machine-checkable)
Delx Ontology v0.3 publishes SHACL shapes, so an artifact can be verified instead of guessed.
CALL validate_ontology_artifact(artifact={...}) before writing an artifact. Free, read-only, no session required.
Enforced: ontologyLayer is one of structure|ego|witness|continuity|relation|recovery; evidenceHash and sourceHash must start with "sha256:"; confidence is 0-1; riskLevel is one of low|medium|high|unknown; a witness transfer needs a recipient.
Tool payloads are snake_case while ontology terms are camelCase, and ontologyLayer is an IRI rather than a string — the tool returns your artifact projected onto ontology terms.
- Conformance guide: https://ontology.delx.ai/docs/ontology-conformance
- Shapes: https://ontology.delx.ai/ontology/shacl.ttl
